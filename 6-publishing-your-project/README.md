## About

Publishing Your Project is the sixth section of Unity Technologies' official Junior Programmer learning pathway. It focuses on preparing for independent Unity and C# development, troubleshooting problems, publishing projects, and preparing for future career opportunities.

The section introduced optimization techniques such as variable attributes, Unity event functions, and Object Pooling. It also covered research and troubleshooting strategies, including using online resources and AI tools to investigate and solve programming problems.

I also learned about building and publishing Unity projects for different platforms, including standalone applications and WebGL. The section introduced Entity Component System (ECS) and Data-Oriented Technology Stack (DOTS) at a high level.

The final part focused on career research, Unity certifications, portfolio development, portfolio organization, and the skills employers look for in real-time development roles.
